# Growth Audit — AntiqSnap

**Live:** https://hribeiro-adapty.github.io/antiqsnap-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas for the AntiqSnap: Antique Identifier onboarding and paywall, plus a clickable prototype rebuilt in AntiqSnap's own design. Only the sequence changes. The art direction stays yours.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
|---|------|-------------------|
| 01 | Offer one month when they tap Cancel | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Name their goal in the paywall headline | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Show a sample valuation before the price | CR +10–20% |
| 04 | Let the loader read their answers back | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Put your 4.7 stars on the paywall | CR +10–15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for AntiqSnap. Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from AntiqSnap's own App Store listing, website and in-app screens. Prices are the US App Store prices.
