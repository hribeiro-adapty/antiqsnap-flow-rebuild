What this is

We opened AntiqSnap the way a first-time collector does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at AntiqSnap: installed AntiqSnap: Antique Identifier, went through the screens up to the paywall, read your App Store listing and antiqsnap.app, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you currently show people.

What follows is five ideas — not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is built from yours — the white splash with your vase icon, the black stage with the dashed scan rings, the hourglass loader, the value-tag table on the paywall, your brown Continue and your plan cards. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment. This is not a design pitch.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us — we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
